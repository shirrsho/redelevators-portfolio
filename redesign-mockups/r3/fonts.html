COMPARE · THE THREE OPTIONS
DECISION · 14 OCT

Schibsted Grotesk + Hanken Grotesk is now the site font. It keeps the calm, professional feel of the current set, but the headlines have more character. Inter is on a huge number of SaaS sites, so the current pairing looks fine but generic. Schibsted was made for a news publisher: it reads like a confident brand without getting in the way of the story.

Bricolage + Figtree is the warmest and most memorable of the three. It fits a hospitality brand, but it’s more opinionated. The headlines get quirky at large sizes, and it will feel a bit less “serious systems company”.

All three are free Google Fonts, so there’s no licensing cost either way.

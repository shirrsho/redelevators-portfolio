Scene 1

The 2am phone

A phone on the nightstand at 2:14am, stacking up guest questions, a commission booking, an empty-nights alert and a locked-out cleaner. As the story turns, each one flips to “Handled for you”. It ends at 7:30am with one message: “Good morning. Nothing needs you.”

  • Strongest at: instant recognition for owner-operators
  • Mood: personal, night into morning
  • B headline: “Still answering guests at 2am?”

Scene 2

The property

The rental itself as an architectural model at dusk. Problems are pinned to real places on the house: the front door, the empty bedroom, the quiet midweek. As they get handled, rooms light up one by one, until it’s blue hour and every room is booked.

  • Strongest at: premium, hospitality feel. Suits property managers too.
  • Mood: architectural, warm, aspirational
  • B headline: “Is your rental running you?”

Scene 3

The calendar

The owner’s real tool: a two-week multi-calendar with an overflowing inbox, OTA fees, open midweek nights and unassigned turnovers. The turn fills the gaps with direct bookings, clears the inbox, adjusts rates and ticks off every turnover, while occupancy climbs.

  • Strongest at: showing the result in numbers they track. Scales to portfolios.
  • Mood: practical, product-like, credible
  • B headline: “Empty nights again this week?”
